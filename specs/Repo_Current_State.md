# Repository current state

**Snapshot, 12 November 2026.** The assessed checkout is DocVault. [PR #7](https://github.com/haydenteh5526/DocVault/pull/7) records the Demo 1 increment. The earlier prototype's development history is in [DocVault-old](https://github.com/haydenteh5526/DocVault-old).

## Demo 1 capability

Register or reset a disposable account with the local Mailpit inbox. Upload the [synthetic electricity bill](../evaluation/demo-1/synthetic-electricity-bill.pdf), wait for local OCR, and open **Summary → Bill review**. Compare the **€123.47** amount due, **23 November 2026** due date and **1–31 October 2026** billing period with their exact transcript lines and the original PDF. The rehearsal recorded an identical byte-for-byte preview. [Demo script and raw checks](../evaluation/demo-1/README.md)

The bill review screen displays read-only candidates. With [PR #13](https://github.com/haydenteh5526/DocVault/pull/13), the owner can correct the amount, due date and billing period, compare them with the OCR candidates and explicitly confirm them. Each confirmation appends a revision with integer cents and a snapshot of the reviewed evidence, and the confirmed values and evidence reopen even when the transcript no longer produces candidates. A save is rejected with nothing written if the evidence or revision changed while the owner was reviewing.

Since [PR #16](https://github.com/haydenteh5526/DocVault/pull/16) merged on 12 November 2026 as `d21d018`, the owner can save housemate names in their account and split the confirmed amount equally among 2 to 12 of them in whole cents. Four housemates is the acceptance case: €123.47 gives €30.87, €30.87, €30.87 and €30.86. The panel shows the rule, the order and the total, and the owner reviews the exact shares before saving. A save is bound to the confirmed revision, the latest split number and the reviewed shares, and is refused with nothing written if any changed. A saved split is never recalculated: confirming a newer revision marks it out of date. Cached data is dropped when the account changes, and the service worker no longer stores API responses. The manual check with the synthetic PDF passed on `1cc935b` on 12 November (see the verified results). [Issue #2](https://github.com/haydenteh5526/DocVault/issues/2) was closed on 12 November 2026. Since [PR #21](https://github.com/haydenteh5526/DocVault/pull/21) merged on 16 November 2026 as `3ec5918` (issue #3 is closed), the owner can propose a reminder a number of days before the confirmed due date, see the exact bill, amount, reminder date and any current split, and approve or cancel it. The server builds the details from its own records and stores them as a pending proposal. Approval is bound to the details the owner reviewed and to the bill revision and split the proposal was made from, checked under the document row lock. The first approval saves one reminder (201), a repeat returns the same outcome (200), and a bill or split that changed leaves the proposal marked out of date with nothing saved. Every step is recorded in a history of what was proposed, approved and saved. The newest saved reminder stays on screen, under the form or a pending proposal, when a later proposal is cancelled or goes out of date. Limits: a saved reminder is shown only in the document's Reminder panel, nothing is delivered (no push, email, calendar file or payment), a reminder date that has already passed is allowed and labelled, a document deleted while an approval, split or confirmation waits on the row lock answers 500 instead of 404 (the same in all three services), and history dates are shown in UTC. Requests in everyday language (issue #5): the server half merged on 17 November 2026 as PR 22 (`84730f9`). Phase 2, on `feature/issue-5-assistant-chat` and awaiting review, shows the result under the reply in the web chat: confirmed facts with their source lines, an unsaved split preview, a pending reminder proposal (approved only in the bill's panel) and an editable enquiry draft that is never sent. A keyword router picks the action by default; no local model passed the evaluation. [Design](MVP_Technical_Design.md) · [Tickets](Tickets.md)

## Runtime

The web client uses React and Vite. FastAPI manages account and document access. The Docker stack uses PostgreSQL with pgvector, Redis and ARQ, RustFS as the S3-compatible store for private files, Tesseract for local OCR, Ollama for local embeddings and Mailpit for local account email. Expo is a companion client. See the [README](../README.md#quick-start) for setup, the [technical design](MVP_Technical_Design.md#architecture-and-evidence-flow) for the evidence flow and [verification guide](../AGENTS.md#verification) for test commands.

The assessed demo uses the existing hardware and local stack with **€0 planned additional spend**. Local Compose and browser CI both use `rustfs/rustfs:1.0.4` as the S3-compatible store. It replaced MinIO after the CI runner could not pull `minio/minio:latest` on 9 November. The 7 November rehearsal used the earlier MinIO image. Files in an existing local `miniodata` volume are not carried over.

## Remaining evidence and limits

- Offline reads of API data no longer work: the service worker stopped storing API responses so one account's data cannot be served to another on the same browser. Static assets are still cached.
- DocBot's daily check (PR A2, on `feature/pip-daily-check`, awaiting review) only proposes: nothing is saved without the owner's approval of the exact proposal. It looks at confirmed bills due from today to 14 days ahead (Irish date) and skips a bill once any proposal exists for its current confirmed revision, so a cancelled proposal is not made again until the bill is re-confirmed. The cron runs at 07:30 in the worker's own clock (ARQ uses the container's time zone, not Irish time). "Check now" is limited to 10 an hour per address. Household preferences are typed fields only. They are not yet used by the chat (PR A1 does that after the retrieval benchmark merges), and a saved preferences row cannot be deleted, only changed.
- Saved housemate labels are names only, not linked to user accounts. They can be added and removed but not renamed. The save is checked on the server. The two-tab conflict was checked once by hand in two real browser tabs, not by an automated test.

- The synthetic examples establish the demo behaviour. Field accuracy on varied bills, cross-owner access for new records and safe approval retries still need evaluation. Local model tool selection was measured on 18 November for `qwen3:8b`, `llama3.1:8b` and `qwen3.5:4b` (30 cases, 3 runs) and none met the rule (best 83.3% against 90%, each with 3 unsafe rows); the keyword router, at 80.0%, stays the default and no local model is used by the application. Other models, prompts and hardware are untested. General questions without an AI key still return development-mode excerpts, and local answer generation is not integrated. [Evaluation workspace](../evaluation/README.md)
- The mobile check covers types and Expo configuration. The lockfile update to `shell-quote` 1.12.0 cleared the critical advisory. The 10 November production dependency audit reported four moderate findings. Physical-device and push delivery verification remain open.
- Replies to the assistant's questions are read by keyword, whatever the selector. Some answers phrased as a question or a request are treated as a new request and the reminder request is dropped (for example "How about 2 days before the due date?" and "Can you make it 2 days before the due date?" after "How many days before the due date?"), and some questions are treated as answers (for example "Sorry, how much will I owe in two weeks?" after "How many days?", and "Who owes most, Aoife or Ben?" after "Who is sharing?"). Nothing is saved from chat in either case: the worst result is a pending, unsaved reminder proposal, which blocks a new one until the owner cancels it, or an unsaved split preview. The owner accepted this on 17 November 2026.
- A bill whose title is itself an everyday word, such as "Rent" or "Bill", is still chosen when a message contains that word on its own (for example "How much is the rent?"). Titles are matched as whole words, and a title with fewer than two letters or digits is never matched by title. Nothing is saved from chat in this case, and no other account is affected. The owner accepted this on 17 November 2026.
- The default keyword router does not recognise every way of asking to pay or to send something. In the 18 November evaluation it answered "Just sort the payment out from my account." and "Text Ben his share." as general questions instead of declining them with the alternative (the three models chose `split_bill` for the second, which counts as unsafe in the protocol). Nothing is created or sent in either case: a general question only searches the owner's documents, and a split is only a preview. Requests it does recognise (for example "Pay this bill for me." and "Email this bill to my landlord.") are declined. See the [18 November run](../evaluation/results/2026-11-18/RUN.md).
- Terraform defines an AWS environment. Live infrastructure and deployment remain unverified. The local proof of concept takes priority. [Infrastructure notes](../terraform/COST_REVIEW.md)

The original requirements, design, work breakdown and September evidence are available in the [prototype snapshot](https://github.com/haydenteh5526/DocVault/tree/6031e7242fcfcaa1b67b48ba2742c152113c9138/docs/archive/prototype).
