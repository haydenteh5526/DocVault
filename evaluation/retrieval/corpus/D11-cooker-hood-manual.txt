Title: Ventis CH90 cooker hood user manual
VENTIS KITCHEN
User manual: Ventis CH90 cooker hood (synthetic example)

1. Using the hood
Switch the hood on before you start cooking and leave it on for 10 minutes
after you finish. Speeds 1 to 3 are for normal cooking, speed 4 is a boost.

2. Filters
Wash the metal grease filter once a month in warm soapy water or in the
dishwasher. Replace the charcoal filter every 6 months if the hood recirculates
air back into the room.

3. Fault codes
Code   Meaning                       What to do
H1     Grease filter saturated       Wash the grease filter and reset
H2     Motor overheated              Switch off for 30 minutes
H3     Light fault                   Replace the lamp, then switch on again

Hold the reset button for 3 seconds to clear a code after you have fixed it.
Helpline: 0000 000 0006 (synthetic number).
