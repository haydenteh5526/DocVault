Title: Aerotherm B24 gas boiler user manual
AEROTHERM HEATING
User manual: Aerotherm B24 gas boiler (synthetic example)

1. Pressure
The pressure gauge should read between 1.0 and 1.5 bar when the boiler is cold.
Top up the system through the filling loop if the pressure drops below 1.0 bar.

2. Heating controls
Set the room thermostat to 20 degrees. Use the timer to switch the heating on
30 minutes before you get up and off 30 minutes before you leave.

3. Service
Have the boiler checked by a registered engineer once a year.
Do not open the sealed case yourself.

4. Fault codes
Code   Meaning                       What to do
F1     Low water pressure            Top up to 1.5 bar and press reset
F28    Ignition failed               Press reset once; call an engineer if
                                     it repeats
F75    Pressure sensor or pump fault Switch off and call an engineer

Smell of gas: turn off the boiler, open the windows and call the emergency
line on 0000 000 0003 (synthetic number).
