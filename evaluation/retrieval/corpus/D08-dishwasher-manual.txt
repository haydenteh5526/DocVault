Title: Nordale DW60 dishwasher user manual
NORDALE KITCHEN
User manual: Nordale DW60 dishwasher (synthetic example)

1. Loading
Load plates facing the centre. Do not block the spray arms.
Use dishwasher salt and rinse aid. Scrape off large food pieces first.

2. Programmes
Eco: 4 hours 10 minutes, 50 degrees.
Auto: 2 hours, 55 degrees.
Quick: 30 minutes, 45 degrees.

3. Care
Clean the filter at the bottom of the tub every two weeks.
Run an empty hot cycle with a dishwasher cleaner every three months.

4. Fault codes
Code   Meaning                       What to do
E01    Water inlet fault             Check the tap and the hose
E15    Leak detected                 Switch off and close the tap
E21    Water temperature too low     Switch off and call the helpline
E24    Drain filter blocked          Clean the filter and the drain hose

If a code stays on the display, switch off at the wall and call the Nordale
helpline on 0000 000 0006 (synthetic number).
