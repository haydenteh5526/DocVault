Title: Vantor WD8-1400 washer-dryer user manual
VANTOR HOME APPLIANCES
User manual: Vantor WD8-1400 washer-dryer (synthetic example)

1. Safety
Do not overload the drum. Disconnect the plug before cleaning. Keep children
and pets away from the door while a programme is running.

2. Capacity
Washing: up to 8 kg of dry cotton.
Drying: up to 5 kg of dry cotton.

3. Care
Clean the lint filter after every drying programme.
The drain filter is behind the lower right panel. Clean it once a month.
Leave the door open after use so that the drum can dry.

4. Fault codes
Code   Meaning                       What to do
E10    Water inlet fault             Check the tap and the hose
E21    Drain pump blocked            Clean the drain filter
E32    Door lock fault               Close the door firmly and restart
E45    Dryer overheating             Clean the lint filter and let it cool
E60    Motor fault                   Switch off and call the helpline

If a code stays on the display after you have followed the steps, switch off
at the wall and call the Vantor helpline on 0000 000 0005 (synthetic number).
