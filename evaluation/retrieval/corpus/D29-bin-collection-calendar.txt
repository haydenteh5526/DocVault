GREENWAY WASTE SERVICES
Collection calendar for Exampletown (synthetic example)

3 November, 17 November, 1 December, 15 December, 29 December, 12 January

Christmas changes
There is no collection on 25 December or 1 January. Collections on those
weeks move to the next working day.
